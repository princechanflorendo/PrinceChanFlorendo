Why Ketheluay?

Some names are chosen because they sound good. Others are chosen because they mean something.

Ketheluay comes from the Subanen language and is associated with expression and an enduring presence. For me, the word captures what I hope this studio represents: the ability of a voice to move beyond the moment in which it is spoken.

A host may only stand on a stage for a few hours. A voiceover may only last a few minutes. Yet the right words, delivered at the right moment, can stay with people long after the microphone has been turned off.

That is what Ketheluay means to me.

It is not simply about having a voice. It is about giving presence to a moment. It is about helping an event become more than a sequence of programs, announcements, and names. It is about creating something that people can hear, feel, and remember.

An enduring voice and presence for your memorable events.
