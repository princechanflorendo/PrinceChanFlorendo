PRINCE CHAN FLORENDO

The voice behind the room

There is more to hosting than speaking into a microphone. It is the ability to read a room, carry its energy, and know when a moment calls for excitement, sincerity, humor, or silence.

My experience in broadcast journalism, event hosting, and live voiceover has shaped the way I approach every engagement. I have learned that a good host does not simply fill the space between program segments. A good host gives the event its rhythm, keeps its audience present, and helps each moment arrive with intention.

Whether I am standing before a live audience or behind a microphone, I bring the same principle into the work: the voice should serve the moment.

Before booking, consider my hosting and voiceover style.

An enduring voice and presence for your memorable events.
